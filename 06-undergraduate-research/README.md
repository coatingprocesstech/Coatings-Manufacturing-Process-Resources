# Undergraduate Research / 本科阶段研究

This section records undergraduate research carried out by Yi Teng at Chongqing University as part of his graduation project. It is kept separate from papers and technical work published during his later professional career.

本分类记录滕毅在重庆大学本科毕业设计期间开展的研究工作，与其职业发展阶段发表的论文及工程技术资料分开归档。

## Research period and background / 研究时间与背景

- **Research and experimental period / 研究与实验时间:** September 1999 to before April 2000（1999年9月以后至2000年4月之前；具体起止日期未进一步细化）
- **Institution / 学校:** Chongqing University / 重庆大学
- **Research context / 研究背景:** Undergraduate graduation project and related graduation thesis / 本科毕业设计及相关毕业论文
- **Research subject / 研究主题:** Silane coupling agents for metallic surface treatment and corrosion protection / 硅烷偶联剂在金属表面处理与防腐蚀中的应用

The undergraduate work involved experimental research related to silane-agent hydrolysis and condensation, solution stability, metal-surface treatment processes, corrosion resistance and coating adhesion. The four related journal papers listed below were published later, in 2001–2002.

本科阶段的研究涉及硅烷试剂的水解与缩聚、溶液稳定性、金属表面处理工艺、耐腐蚀性能及涂层附着力等实验内容。下列四篇相关期刊论文于后续的2001—2002年发表。

## Research collaboration / 研究合作背景

One of the related papers, “硅烷试剂防腐蚀工艺研究” (《材料保护》, 2001, 34(11)), lists Wang Nan, Xu Yi, Yang Lihua and Yi Teng as its authors. According to Yi Teng's recollection, Prof. Wang Nan was collaborating technically with Prof. Xu Yi at the time, and Yang Lihua was a student supervised by Prof. Wang Nan.

其中，《硅烷试剂防腐蚀工艺研究》（《材料保护》2001年第11期）署名为王楠、徐溢、杨立华、滕毅。根据滕毅的回忆，当时王楠老师与徐溢教授存在技术合作，杨立华是王楠老师指导的学生。
