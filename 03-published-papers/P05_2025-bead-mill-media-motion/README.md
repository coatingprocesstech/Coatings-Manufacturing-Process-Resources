## English overview

The paper analyses how fluid rheology affects grinding-media motion through fluid resistance, and how this influences media kinetic energy, momentum and impulse. It discusses spindle speed, media size, density and loading ratio, and introduces a fluid–solid interaction framework for understanding grinding.

## 中文简介

从经典动力学与流变学角度分析不同流体环境下研磨介质的运动机制，讨论流体阻力对介质动能、动量和冲量的影响，以及主轴转速、介质粒径、密度和装填率等参数对研磨效率和效果的影响。
