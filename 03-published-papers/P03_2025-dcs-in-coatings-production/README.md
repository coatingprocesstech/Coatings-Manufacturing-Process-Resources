## English overview

The paper discusses the application prospects of distributed control systems in coatings production, including process control, productivity and product quality, DCS/PLC investment considerations and future directions connected with IoT and AI.

## 中文简介

讨论 DCS 在涂料生产中的应用前景，涉及生产过程控制、生产效率和产品质量，并比较 DCS 与 PLC 的投资回报考虑，结合物联网和人工智能讨论未来趋势。
