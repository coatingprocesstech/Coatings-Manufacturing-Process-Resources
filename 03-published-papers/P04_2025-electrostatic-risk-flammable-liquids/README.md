## English overview

The paper reviews electrostatic charge generation and accumulation during the flow and transfer of flammable liquids, with attention to high-flow conditions. It discusses flow-rate control, charge dissipation and grounding, material selection, pipeline design and integrated electrostatic-risk management.

分析易燃液体在流动和输送过程中的静电产生与积聚风险，讨论流速控制、静电消散与接地、材料选择、管道设计及综合风险管理措施。
