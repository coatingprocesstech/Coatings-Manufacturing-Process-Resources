# Journal-Arranged Paper-Explainer Video Courses / 期刊编辑部安排的论文讲解视频课

These are two slide decks prepared for video-course explanations arranged by the editorial office of **Modern Paint & Finishing (《现代涂料与涂装》)**. They are not general training lectures or conference presentations.

- V01 explains published paper P01 on the digital-transformation strategy for the coatings industry.
- V02 explains published paper P05 on grinding-media motion mechanisms in bead mills.

Each slide deck remains a separate resource and is directly linked to its corresponding original journal PDF.
