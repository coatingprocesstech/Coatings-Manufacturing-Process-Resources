## English overview

A conference presentation on capability development in coatings manufacturing: automated production, data-supported process control and model-assisted intervention. Using wet grinding as an example, it emphasizes that process data must be reliable, traceable and connected to material and process conditions before supporting process windows, warning limits, intervention limits or models.
