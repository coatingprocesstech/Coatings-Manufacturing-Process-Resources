## English overview

The paper compares VOC definitions and calculation rules in China, the European Union and the United States. It discusses solvent power, evaporation behaviour and residual solvent in high-solids coatings, and emphasizes that VOC-exempt status does not establish low health, safety or environmental risk.

## 中文简介

比较中国、欧盟和美国 VOC 定义及核算规则，讨论高固体分涂料中豁免溶剂的溶解力、挥发行为和涂膜残留，并进行总风险评估。
